# CONNECT FOR BRANDS partner console — module author guide

Product: a Stripe-quality self-serve console for **partners** (manufacturers/distributors of IoT devices: inverters, GPS trackers, alarms, boiler relays…).
The partner registers, creates brands → categories → device models, uploads IMEI whitelists, orders **Activation Keys** and **Service Tokens** from OTA TECHNOLOGY DMCC (USD Tax Invoices), receives **SIM cards in custody** via shipments, manages SIM inventory, generates activation codes and hands them to resellers (installers, shops, branches), and plans revenue.
We (OTA) set the plan per model (Light / Standard / Premium) and the per-country price lists. In the demo this is done in **"OTA mode (demo)"** (`S.admin === true`, toggle in the sidebar). Show admin-only controls only when `S.admin` and mark them with `ui.note('admin', …)` or the violet badge so the demo audience understands they're our-side actions.

## Files & build
- Your module: `src/modules/<NN>_<name>.js` (and optional `src/modules/<NN>_<name>.css`). Do NOT edit `src/01_core.js`, `src/02_shared.js`, `src/00_core.css`, `src/99_boot.js` or other agents' modules. If you need a core change, describe it at the end of your report.
- Build: `python3 build.py` → `index.html` + `bundle.js`. Check: `node --check bundle.js`.
- Smoke test: a static server already runs on http://localhost:8777 serving this folder (if not: `cd <console> && python3 -m http.server 8777 &`). Use Playwright (`require('playwright')`, chromium preinstalled; viewport 1280×800 and 390×844). Click `[data-act=demo]` on the signup page (or call `seedDemo()` via your own flow) to get demo data. Make sure: no `pageerror`, no horizontal overflow at 390px (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth <= 0`), all your routes render in uk/en/ru.
- All files are concatenated into ONE IIFE in this order: 01_core.js, 02_shared.js, modules sorted by filename, 99_boot.js. Everything is a plain global inside that IIFE. Top-level names must not collide: prefix every top-level function/const in your module with your module prefix (e.g. `cat_`, `ord_`).

- Clear page header with one primary action; summary before detail; tables with status badges, search/filter, pagination for long lists (50/page); empty states that explain the next step with a button; drawers for detail; inline validation with specific messages; toasts that say what happened ("Invoice CFB-2026-0005 marked as paid").

## Business rules (shared truth) — commercial model v09 + legal pack v10
- **Seller**: OTA TECHNOLOGY DMCC invoices the partner ONLY in **USD** (`S.ota` data, frozen into each invoice as `inv.seller`). No UAH, no Ukrainian entity, no currency choice (`S.org.currency` is always `'USD'`).
- **What is sold**: **Activation Keys** ("copies of the CONNECT computer program (licence activation keys)", one per device for life, not linked to SIM shipments) and **Service Tokens** (plan × term: Light/Standard/Premium × 12/24/36/48/60 months; valid **36 months from issue**; exchangeable). **SIM cards are never sold or invoiced.**
- **Prices**: per-country, versioned `S.priceLists[CC]` (USD net): key price, tokens 3×5, OTA Retained Amount per paid month (3 plans), compliance fee 0–3%. Seed (LEGAL_SPEC §1): UA key $9.85, tokens = (75 / 99 UAH ÷ 1.2 ÷ 45.0564) × months × (1 − disc), disc 12m 10%, 24m 20%, 36m 30%, 48m 35%, 60m 40%; Premium = Standard × 1.5; retained = monthly net wholesale. KZ: key $9.40, 990 / 1 290 KZT ÷ 1.16 ÷ 441.77. Fee 0%. Any increase (price, retained, fee) is effective **≥ 90 days** after the change (`plAdd` clamps `from`); decreases from the chosen date. **v10 (ТЗ 3.2):** key / token / retained prices may not rise more than **10% in any rolling 12 months** (base = lowest value in force during the 12 months before `from`; `plIncreaseCheck(cc,data,from)` → `{increase,minFrom,noticeOk,cap:[…]}`; `plAdd` returns `{err:'cap'}` and saves nothing; `plCheckVersion(cc,ver)` re-checks a version before its Order Form version is issued). An increase needs a **justification** (supplier / regulatory cost growth) and a **document link** (`v.justification`, `v.docLink`; UI passes `strict:true` → `{err:'justification'}` if missing; non-strict callers get `v.justMissing`). The fee (0–3% rate) counts as an increase for the notice but is not measured against the 10% cap. An increase never applies to keys, tokens or subscription periods already paid (invoices/lots freeze unit prices and `priceVer`). A new version withdraws still-pending ones. Orders/drafts are priced from the version current when the invoice is issued.
- **Orders**: a checkout creates a **Key order** (`K-0001`) and/or a **Token order** (`T-0001`) for one country, on **one combined invoice or two**. Minimums per partner (`orgMin()`): first key order ≥ 1 000 keys, later ≥ 100 (OTA edits on Prices). Tokens only for plans assigned to approved models. Drafts (no invoice, no number used) can be deleted; issued orders are cancelled via a **credit note**. `orderBlock(cc)` (HOOKS.orderBlock) can block a country. A price-changed Order Form blocks only its own country after the effective date (legal module). **v10 (ТЗ 3.5):** instead of signing, the partner signatory may **terminate the Order Form for that country** (`ofTerminate(cc,{priceVer,note})` → notice `{type:'of_terminate'}` in `S.notices`, `S.org.ofTerminated[cc]`, audit `orderform.terminate`); new orders for that country are then blocked (core orderBlock hook), other countries unaffected.
- **Invoices** (`mkInvoice`, template 13): "TAX INVOICE", number `OTA-INV-YYYY-NNNNNN` (global), USD; header "Registration no. {OTA_REGNO} · Licence no. {OTA_LICENCE}" (service licence only, demo values fictional) + TRN; lines exactly "Activation Keys – copies of the CONNECT computer program (licence activation keys) – {Approved Country}" and "Service Tokens {Plan}, {term} months". **No 5% VAT anywhere (ТЗ 1.1):** GCC partners cannot register, `invVat()` is always 0%: ONE line "VAT 0% – zero-rated export of services (Article 31, Executive Regulation of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on VAT)" 0.00, plus the mandatory line "VAT amount in AED at the UAE Central Bank rate {AED_RATE} on the invoice date — AED 0.00". The rate comes from `S.fx.aed` (`[{date,usd_aed,src}]`, demo 3.6725 'CBUAE (demo)', edited by OTA Finance `ota.vat` on the Invoices page in OTA mode, `aedRate(date)`, `aedRateSet`) and is stored with the document (`inv.aedRate`, `inv.aedRateDate`, `inv.aedSrc`). Statuses `open → paid | cancelled`; overdue is derived (`invStatus`). Partner can report "I've paid".
- **Issue after payment**: only **OTA Finance** (`can('ota.payments')`) marks paid → `invoicePaid()` creates key lots / token lots and completes exchanges automatically. Nothing is issued before.
- **Tax Credit Notes** (template **13A** `LEGAL_TPL.CN`, `OTA-CN-YYYY-NNNNNN`, global counter, never reused — also across demo resets): `creditNote(inv, reason, {settle})`, reason ∈ `CN_REASONS` = `cancel_unpaid` (unpaid only) | `error` | `refund` (paid only) with the ТЗ 2.3 EN/RU texts. Lines = invoice lines (stored negative in `cn.lines`; the print template gets unsigned amounts and prints "–"), VAT 0%, "VAT adjustment in AED at the UAE Central Bank rate {AED_RATE} — AED –0.00" (rate of the CN date stored in `cn.aedRate`). Unpaid source → invoice `cancelled`, no keys/tokens ever issued under it (`invoicePaid` refuses). Paid source (only while none of its keys/tokens is used) → lots revoked, `cn.settle` = `refund` (to partner's bank) | `setoff` (next order). Shape `{id,number,invoiceId,invoiceNo,invoiceDate,lines,net,vat:0,total,aedRate,aedRateDate,reason,reasonNote,wasPaid,settle,createdAt,by}`. Invoices are never deleted.
- **Numbering (ТЗ 2.1):** RS per country `OTA-ARV-RS-UA-202611`; INV/CN global; PF/SR same NNNN; partner commission invoice uses the partner's numbering and may reference **one or more statements of several countries of the month** ("per Statement(s) …", `ci.rsIds` / `ci.rsNos`, template key `RS_NO`).
- **Settlement deadlines (ТЗ 2.8, MPA 8.2):** OTA statement by the 10th, partner invoice by the 15th, payment of undisputed invoices by both by the 20th; a statement without the partner's answer within 5 business days is confirmed automatically (`ops_autoConfirm`).
- **Activation (ТЗ 3.1)** — `deviceActivate(sim, {via:'token', code} | {via:'subscription', sub})`: the **Activation Key** is consumed at activation (one per device for life). The plan comes **either** from a Service Token (the code: 1 token of the exact plan+term, soonest-expiring lot first) **or** from an **active Direct Subscription** (`S.subs[]` `{id,iccid,imei,plan,cc,status,startedAt,paidUntil,autoRenew,src:'card'}`, `subCreate`, `subActive`) — then no token is used. No key → rejected (`no_key`), code stays valid. Codes redeemed in the customer portal (bridge `cfb-demo-ownership.codes[CODE].status='redeemed'`, `device_id` = IMEI) are activated by `cx_bridgeSync` with the same rules. Expired tokens are shown but unusable. A code's plan must match the model's plan; only approved models.
- **Exchange** (`exchangeCreate`): N tokens A → B (same validity). More expensive → difference invoice (`kind:'exchange'`, line type `xchg`), tokens reserved until paid; cheaper or equal → immediate, no refund.
- **SIM shipments in custody — only from the EU (ТЗ 1.2)**: SIMs never enter or transit the UAE. Each shipment has `ship_from{name,addr,country (EU)}`, `ship_from_country`, `exporter{name,eori}` (EU exporter of record acting for OTA; demo values fictional). PF (11) shows "Owner of the goods: OTA TECHNOLOGY DMCC" (no TRN), ship-from address, exporter + EORI, country of dispatch / destination; A2 box label print view `#opsdoc--a2--id` shows "SHIPPER (OWNER OF GOODS) — OTA TECHNOLOGY DMCC" + SHIP FROM block. **No Dubai Customs code** (`OTA_CUSTOMS_CODE` removed everywhere). Demo ICCIDs = `89380012` + 10-digit index + Luhn digit (19 digits, `genIccid`, `iccidIndex`). OTA Admin creates a shipment (qty, first ICCID, SIM type, customs unit value, DAP address/importer, ship-from, exporter/EORI) → `OTA-PF-YYYY-NNNN` + `OTA-SR-YYYY-NNNN` (same NNNN) → `prepared → shipped (carrier, waybill) → delivered → received`. Partner (Admin/Operations, `p.receipt`) confirms received qty, packaging condition, storage place, responsible person → SIMs enter `S.sims` (status `stock` = in custody) with `shipmentId`. Custody covers only uninstalled SIMs.
- **Direct subscriptions**: commission = Net Collected Revenue (minus OTA taxes in customer country, acquiring, refunds, compliance fee) − OTA Retained Amount × paid months. RS by the 10th, commission invoice by the 15th (legal/ops modules). The calculator keeps local-currency retail but uses USD wholesale × FX.
- **Annual target** 3 000 activated devices (not a purchase obligation).
- IMEI whitelist, model plan review, SIM statuses `stock` / `active` / `blocked` as before; SIMs carry `keyLotId` once their device consumed a key.
