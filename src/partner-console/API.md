# CONNECT FOR BRANDS partner console — module author guide

Product: a Stripe-quality self-serve console for **partners** (manufacturers/distributors of IoT devices: inverters, GPS trackers, alarms, boiler relays…).
The partner registers, creates brands → categories → device models, uploads IMEI whitelists, orders **Activation Keys** and **Service Tokens** from OTA TECHNOLOGY DMCC (USD Tax Invoices), receives **SIM cards in custody** via shipments, manages SIM inventory, generates activation codes and hands them to resellers (installers, shops, branches), and plans revenue.
We (OTA) set the plan per model (Light / Standard / Premium) and the per-country price lists. In the demo this is done in **"OTA mode (demo)"** (`S.admin === true`, toggle in the sidebar). Show admin-only controls only when `S.admin` and mark them with `ui.note('admin', …)` or the violet badge so the demo audience understands they're our-side actions.

## Files & build
- Your module: `src/modules/<NN>_<name>.js` (and optional `src/modules/<NN>_<name>.css`). Do NOT edit `src/01_core.js`, `src/02_shared.js`, `src/00_core.css`, `src/99_boot.js` or other agents' modules. If you need a core change, describe it at the end of your report.
- Build: `python3 build.py` → `index.html` + `bundle.js`. Check: `node --check bundle.js`.
- Smoke test: a static server already runs on http://localhost:8777 serving this folder (if not: `cd <console> && python3 -m http.server 8777 &`). Use Playwright (`require('playwright')`, chromium preinstalled; viewport 1280×800 and 390×844). Click `[data-act=demo]` on the signup page (or call `seedDemo()` via your own flow) to get demo data. Make sure: no `pageerror`, no horizontal overflow at 390px (`document.documentElement.scrollWidth - innerWidth <= 0`), all your routes render in uk/en/ru.
- All files are concatenated into ONE IIFE in this order: 01_core.js, 02_shared.js, modules sorted by filename, 99_boot.js. Everything is a plain global inside that IIFE. Top-level names must not collide: prefix every top-level function/const in your module with your module prefix (e.g. `cat_`, `ord_`).

## Quality bar ("Stripe level")
- Clear page header with one primary action; summary before detail; tables with status badges, search/filter, pagination for long lists (50/page); empty states that explain the next step with a button; drawers for detail; inline validation with specific messages; toasts that say what happened ("Invoice CFB-2026-0005 marked as paid").
- Plain language for a busy business owner. No jargon where a normal word works. Never invent legal facts; placeholders in [brackets].
- Works at 390px width (tables scroll inside `.tblwrap`), dark mode via tokens only (never literal colors except brand colors the partner picks), keyboard accessible (real buttons/links/labels).
- Keep it fast: S.sims can be thousands, S.codes thousands — paginate and avoid O(n²) loops in render.

## Business rules (shared truth) — commercial model v09
- **Seller**: OTA TECHNOLOGY DMCC invoices the partner ONLY in **USD** (`S.ota` data, frozen into each invoice as `inv.seller`). No UAH, no Ukrainian entity, no currency choice (`S.org.currency` is always `'USD'`).
- **What is sold**: **Activation Keys** ("copies of the CONNECT computer program (licence activation keys)", one per device for life, not linked to SIM shipments) and **Service Tokens** (plan × term: Light/Standard/Premium × 12/24/36/48/60 months; valid **36 months from issue**; exchangeable). **SIM cards are never sold or invoiced.**
- **Prices**: per-country, versioned `S.priceLists[CC]` (USD net): key price, tokens 3×5, OTA Retained Amount per paid month (3 plans), compliance fee 0–3%. Seed (LEGAL_SPEC §1): UA key $9.85, tokens = (75 / 99 UAH ÷ 1.2 ÷ 45.0564) × months × (1 − disc), disc 12m 10%, 24m 20%, 36m 30%, 48m 35%, 60m 40%; Premium = Standard × 1.5; retained = monthly net wholesale. KZ: key $9.40, 990 / 1 290 KZT ÷ 1.16 ÷ 441.77. Fee 0%. Any increase (price, retained, fee) is effective **≥ 90 days** after the change (`plAdd` clamps `from`); decreases from the chosen date. A new version withdraws still-pending ones. Orders/drafts are priced from the version current when the invoice is issued.
- **Orders**: a checkout creates a **Key order** (`K-0001`) and/or a **Token order** (`T-0001`) for one country, on **one combined invoice or two**. Minimums per partner (`orgMin()`): first key order ≥ 1 000 keys, later ≥ 100 (OTA edits on Prices). Tokens only for plans assigned to approved models. Drafts (no invoice, no number used) can be deleted; issued orders are cancelled via a **credit note**. `orderBlock(cc)` (HOOKS.orderBlock) can block a country.
- **Invoices** (`mkInvoice`): title "Tax Invoice", number `OTA-INV-YYYY-NNNNNN` (global), USD; lines exactly "Activation Keys – copies of the CONNECT computer program (licence activation keys)" (+ Approved Country shown separately) and "Service Tokens [Plan], [term] months"; VAT by `S.org.country` (registration): ≠ AE → 0% with "VAT 0% – zero-rated export of services (Article 31 of the Executive Regulation of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on VAT)"; AE → 5% + `inv.vatWarn` (audit + log for OTA Finance). Statuses `open → paid | cancelled`; overdue is derived (`invStatus`). Partner can report "I've paid".
- **Issue after payment**: only **OTA Finance** (`can('ota.payments')`) marks paid → `invoicePaid()` creates key lots / token lots and completes exchanges automatically. Nothing is issued before.
- **Credit notes** `OTA-CN-YYYY-NNNNNN`: cancel an open invoice (partner cancels order, or OTA Finance), or a paid one only while none of its keys/tokens are used (lots revoked). Invoices are never deleted; numbers never reused.
- **Activation**: each real activation consumes **1 Service Token of the exact plan+term** (soonest-expiring valid lot first) and, if the device (IMEI) has no key yet, **1 Activation Key**. No token or no key → rejected, code stays valid. Expired tokens are shown but unusable. A code's plan must match the model's plan; only approved models.
- **Exchange** (`exchangeCreate`): N tokens A → B (same validity). More expensive → difference invoice (`kind:'exchange'`, line type `xchg`), tokens reserved until paid; cheaper or equal → immediate, no refund.
- **SIM shipments in custody**: OTA Admin creates a shipment (qty, first ICCID, SIM type, customs unit value, DAP address/importer) → `OTA-PF-YYYY-NNNN` + `OTA-SR-YYYY-NNNN` (same NNNN) → `prepared → shipped (carrier, waybill) → delivered → received`. Partner (Admin/Operations, `p.receipt`) confirms received qty, packaging condition, storage place, responsible person → SIMs enter `S.sims` (status `stock` = in custody) with `shipmentId`. Custody covers only uninstalled SIMs.
- **Direct subscriptions**: commission = Net Collected Revenue (minus OTA taxes in customer country, acquiring, refunds, compliance fee) − OTA Retained Amount × paid months. RS by the 10th, commission invoice by the 15th (legal/ops modules). The calculator keeps local-currency retail but uses USD wholesale × FX.
- **Annual target** 3 000 activated devices (not a purchase obligation).
- IMEI whitelist, model plan review, SIM statuses `stock` / `active` / `blocked` as before; SIMs carry `keyLotId` once their device consumed a key.
